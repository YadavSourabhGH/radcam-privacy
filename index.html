RadCam Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 October 2026
App developer: ArclineLabs
App package: com.arcline.radcam
Contact: arclinelabs@gmail.com

This policy describes how RadCam for Android handles information when you capture, import, edit, save or share photos, send feedback or choose to support the developer.

Your photos

RadCam processes camera images and selected photos on your device. It does not upload your photos to ArclineLabs or a remote editing service. The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs and does not request Internet access.

Permissions and local storage

Camera
With your permission, RadCam shows a camera preview and captures photos. Camera access is optional for importing and editing images. You can revoke it in Android Settings.
Selected photos
RadCam reads images you deliberately choose through Android's document picker. It does not request broad photo-library access.
Saved photos and preferences
Camera captures are saved to Pictures/RadCam and to Studio in app-private storage. Edited exports are saved as new copies. The editor keeps a working draft and your editing settings locally. Camera preferences, such as aspect ratio and grid visibility, are also saved locally. Capturing a photo does not replace an editor draft.
Template samples
Template previews use sample photographs bundled with the app. They do not require downloading photos or reading your library.

Sharing photos

When you tap Share and choose another app, Android grants that app temporary read-only access to the selected JPEG or selected JPEGs. RadCam does not send the photo automatically. The destination app's policies and your actions there govern subsequent use.

Feedback and bug reports

Feedback opens your email app with ArclineLabs' contact address. Bug-report drafts include the RadCam version, Android version and device manufacturer/model to help diagnose a problem. You can review and change the draft before sending it. Nothing is sent until you choose to send it in your email app. ArclineLabs receives the address, message and any attachments you send, and uses them to respond to your request and investigate issues.

Optional developer support

You may choose an optional contribution through the supplied Razorpay or PayPal links. This opens the provider in your browser or another app. All RadCam features remain available regardless of a contribution. RadCam does not process or store payment-card details, bank information or UPI credentials. The payment provider handles information you submit according to its own policies. ArclineLabs may receive transaction and contact information made available by the provider to manage contributions and related support.

Provider policies: Razorpay and PayPal.

Retention and deletion

Photos in shared device storage remain until you remove them through your gallery or file manager. Removing a photo from RadCam's Studio removes its app-private copy and leaves its separately saved gallery copy. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes private photos, working drafts and preferences, but may leave shared gallery copies. Android backups, cloud galleries, document providers and other apps operate under their own settings and policies.

Email correspondence and contribution records may be retained as needed to answer requests, investigate issues and meet applicable recordkeeping obligations. Contact ArclineLabs for questions or deletion requests concerning information you have sent directly; third-party provider records are governed by those providers.

Children and families

RadCam supports on-device photography and editing for families. It does not require a child to create an account, provide a name, email address or date of birth, or allow advertising or analytics tracking. Camera images and selected photos stay on the device unless a user deliberately shares them through another app. Parents and guardians should supervise children’s use of photo sharing, feedback and external contribution services. Please avoid sending information about a child in feedback unless it is necessary to answer your request. A parent or guardian can contact ArclineLabs about information sent directly to us.

Contact and updates

ArclineLabs does not sell personal information through RadCam. Contact arclinelabs@gmail.com with feedback, bug reports or privacy questions. If the app's practices change, this policy and its effective date will be updated.
